I am a Mathematics undergraduate at the University of Warwick, with a research focus on the mathematical theory of machine learning. My interests include neural-network theory, optimisation dynamics, infinite-width limits, hyperparameter transfer, and scaling laws.

I am currently a member of Professor Fanghui Liu's research group, where I collaborate with Yikuan Li on learning-rate schedule transfer across scales under μP. I am also beginning to explore the mathematical relationship between checkpoint averaging and learning-rate decay under the guidance of Professor Ranko Lazić.

In addition to my research, I was appointed as a Graduate Teaching Assistant (GTA) at Warwick's Mathematics Institute during my undergraduate studies, where I lead small-group mathematics supervisions.

I am interested in the nonlinear dynamics and scaling limits of neural-network training. Using spectral analysis, perturbation theory, and asymptotic methods, I study the stability of optimisation trajectories and how network width, training steps, and learning-rate strategies shape the training process. My interests also include stochastic processes, Brownian motion, and related stochastic differential equations, particularly the effects of stochastic perturbations on training dynamics and optimisation.

Research Interests

Neural-Network Theory

Rigorous properties of learning models, including optimisation, generalisation, asymptotic limits, and scaling behaviour.

Optimisation Trajectories and Training Dynamics

Training trajectories, optimisation stability, and the relationships between learning-rate strategies, viewed through dynamical systems, spectral analysis, and stochastic processes.

Infinite-Width Limits and μP

Parametrisation, feature learning, infinite-width and deterministic limits, and the cross-width stability of hyperparameters.

Hyperparameter Transfer and Scaling Laws

Learning-rate schedules and the transfer of their optimal parameters across network widths and training steps.

Research Experience

Understanding how neural networks train, and how learning rates transfer across scales.

Previous Research · URSS 2026

Two-Step Optimal Learning-Rate Geometry under μP

Supervised by Professor Fanghui Liu · May 2026 – July 2026

Studied the global geometry of optimal learning rates in a two-step deep linear μP network. Proved generic uniqueness, constructed full-rank examples with three distinct global optima, and established stability under perturbations.
