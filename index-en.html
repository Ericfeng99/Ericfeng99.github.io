About

I am a Mathematics undergraduate at the University of Warwick, with a research focus on the mathematical theory of machine learning. My interests include neural-network theory, optimisation dynamics, infinite-width limits, hyperparameter transfer, and scaling laws.

I am currently a member of Professor Fanghui Liu's research group, where I study learning-rate schedule transfer across scales under μP. I am also beginning to explore the mathematical relationship between checkpoint averaging and learning-rate decay under the guidance of Professor Ranko Lazić.

I am particularly interested in using probability, linear algebra, optimisation, and asymptotic analysis to develop rigorous and verifiable mathematical explanations of neural-network training.

Previous Research · URSS 2026

Two-Step Optimal Learning-Rate Geometry under μP

Supervised by Professor Fanghui Liu · 2026

Studied the global geometry of optimal learning rates in a two-step deep linear μP network. Proved generic uniqueness, constructed full-rank examples with three distinct global optima, and established stability under perturbations.
