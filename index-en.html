I am a Mathematics undergraduate at the University of Warwick, working on the mathematical theory of machine learning. My interests include neural-network training dynamics, infinite-width limits, hyperparameter transfer, and scaling laws.

I am a member of Professor Fanghui Liu’s research group, where I study learning-rate schedule transfer across scales under μP. I am also beginning to explore checkpoint averaging and learning-rate decay with Professor Ranko Lazić.

I also serve as a Teaching Assistant at Warwick’s Mathematics Institute, leading small-group mathematics supervisions for first-year MORSE students.

Supervised by Fanghui Liu

Mathematical foundations of learning-rate schedule transfer across network widths and training budgets, with a focus on optimisation dynamics, scaling behaviour, and μP.

Checkpoint Averaging and Learning-Rate Decay

Supervised by Ranko Lazić

Exploring the mathematical relationship between checkpoint averaging and learning-rate decay, and its implications for training dynamics and learning-rate selection.

URSS 2026May – July 2026

Two-Step Optimal Learning-Rate Geometry under μP

Supervised by Fanghui Liu

Studied optimal learning-rate geometry in a two-step deep linear μP network, proving generic uniqueness, constructing full-rank examples with three distinct global optima, and establishing stability under perturbations.
