About

I am an undergraduate student in Mathematics at the University of Warwick, with academic interests in quantum computing, mathematical foundations of machine learning, and combinatorial optimisation.

My current work focuses on using linear algebraic and structural methods to study problems in quantum information and machine learning, including stabilizer formalism, symplectic linear algebra and graph-theoretic optimisation.

This website collects selected essays, projects, notes, and academic materials.

Research Interests

  • Quantum computing and stabilizer formalism
  • Symplectic linear algebra and quantum information
  • Mathematical foundations of machine learning
  • Combinatorial optimisation and graph-theoretic methods
